Phone access

Copies your deadlines, grades and ticks to a secret GitHub gist that the phone page reads.

  1. Make a fine-grained token at github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new. Under Account permissions, set Gists to Read and write. Nothing else is needed.
  2. Paste it below and press Save. Leave the gist blank and one is made for you.
  3. On your phone, open your phone page and enter the gist ID it shows here (and the token, if you want to tick things off from the phone).
