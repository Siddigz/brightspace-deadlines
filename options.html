Phone access

Copies your deadlines, grades and ticks to a private GitHub repo that the phone page reads.

  1. Make a new private repo at github.com/new. Leave it empty.
  2. Make a fine-grained token at github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick that repo. Under Permissions, set Contents to Read and write. Pick a long expiry; when it runs out, make a new one and enter it here and on the phone.
  3. Enter the repo and token below and press Save.
  4. On your phone, open your phone page and enter the same repo and token.
